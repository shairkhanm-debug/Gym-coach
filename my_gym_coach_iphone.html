Nutrition basics

  • A practical protein range for many active adults is roughly 1.2–1.6 g/kg/day; at 77 kg that is about 92–123 g daily. You do not need to force a very high target.
  • Include protein across meals, vegetables/fruit, and enough carbohydrates to support training.
  • Use a modest calorie deficit if fat loss is the goal; avoid crash diets.
  • Drink water regularly, especially in Kuwait's heat and around exercise.

Your weekly split

12-week progression

  • Weeks 1–2 — Learn: Choose easy-to-moderate loads, leave 2–3 good reps in reserve, focus on safe technique.
  • Weeks 3–4 — Build consistency: Add reps within the range while keeping form steady.
  • Weeks 5–8 — Gradual progression: When all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form, increase weight slightly next time.
  • Weeks 9–11 — Keep quality high: Progress only if recovery is good. Do not train to failure on every set.
  • Week 12 — Review: Compare measurements, strength, energy, and adherence. Repeat or adjust based on results.

If you feel unusually fatigued or your joints ache, reduce sets/loads or take an extra recovery day.

Warm-up and cardio

  • Warm up 5–10 minutes with easy walking/cycling, then do 1–3 lighter warm-up sets before the first big lift.
  • Cardio day: 30–40 minutes at a moderate pace where you can still speak in sentences.
  • Optional easy 10–20 minute walks on 2–3 other days if recovery allows.
  • Cool down gently and stretch comfortably; no painful forcing.
